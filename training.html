🧭 First shift path

A simple route for trainees: learn the rules, practise one station, get trainer feedback, then build confidence during quieter times.

3Practise slowly

Accuracy first, then speed. Do not rush unsafe work.

📋 Trainee basics

Rules new crew should remember

These are general UK fast-food workplace habits. Always follow your store trainer, manager, and official workplace documents.

  • ⏰Be on time. Arrive ready, not just inside the building at start time.
  • 🧼Wash hands often. Especially after breaks, cleaning, toilets, bins, or changing tasks.
  • 🗣️Ask when unsure. Guessing on food safety, tills, or customer issues is a bad plan.
  • 🤝Respect the team. No shouting, no mocking, no drama in front of customers.
🇬🇧 UK crew starter pack

Things trainees usually learn in UK stores

Use this as a practical learning checklist. Exact rules can change by store and company policy.

🧢 Uniform clean and presentable 📱 Phone away during work 🧾 Ask manager for refund/complaint help 🧽 Clean as you go 🥤 Breaks follow rota/manager plan 🚪 Tell manager before leaving station 🧯 Report hazards quickly 🍔 Allergen questions go to trained person/manager
Safety note: This app should not replace official food safety, allergen, fire safety, cash handling, or HR training. If unsure, ask your trainer or manager.

💬 How to sound confident

Useful trainee phrases

  • 🙋“Can you show me that once more, please?”
  • ✅“I understand the first part, but I need help with the next step.”
  • 👥“Can I get a manager to help with this?”
  • 🧼“I’m going to wash my hands before switching task.”
