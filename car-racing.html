Ready?

Use A/D or arrow keys to steer. Press W/Up to accelerate, S/Down to brake, and Space for nitro.

How to play

  • Move: A/D or ←/→
  • Speed: W/↑ to accelerate, S/↓ to brake
  • Nitro: Space or ⚡ button
  • Goal: avoid red cars, collect coins, beat your best score
Pro tip: Do not spam nitro. Save it for tight gaps when traffic gets evil. Tiny goblin road behaviour, basically.
